Why Remembrance exists

Memory should feel alive.

Most digital memories are buried in chronological grids. Remembrance treats them as relationships: one song can connect to a city, one person to a season, one photograph to a feeling you almost forgot. The goal is not to store more. It is to help you notice what your life already contains.

The interface is intentionally calm, highly readable and touch-friendly so the archive can be used by children, adults and older family members without learning a complicated system.
